Storyline taken from 2024 revised uncensored chinese version available on JJXWC.

Continuing from the [previous post](/projects/ascension/), I wanted to convert my lua love2D game into a 3D game made with Unreal Engine and C++.

Instead of limiting myself to a little minigame, I aimed for the moon and decided to start off with making the game from the first chapter and stopping when I decided I had burnt myself out.

Some tech I used:

- Unreal Engine 5
- C++ 20
- maya for modelling
- substance painter
- substance designer
- blender
